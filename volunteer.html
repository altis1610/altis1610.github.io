Volunteer Tutoring, Li-Ming Institute

Teaching arithmetic through images instead of symbols.

BRIDGING SYMBOLS AND UNDERSTANDING

Teaching addition and subtraction to learners with intellectual disabilities, I found bare digits would not stick — so I turned "7" into a giraffe's neck and "2" into a small duck. Once the symbols carried images and stories, recognition and memory improved markedly; then I gradually removed the pictures to guide understanding back to the abstract.

WHY IT MATTERED

Years later, building AphaSpeak, I realized I was still working on the same problem: building correspondences between forms of data so broken messages can be reconstructed into meaning.
