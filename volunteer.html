Volunteer Tutoring, Li-Ming Institute

Taught arithmetic to learners with intellectual disabilities, using images in place of symbols.

The method

I taught addition and subtraction to learners with intellectual disabilities. Bare digits would not stick, so I redrew them as images — "7" as a giraffe's neck, "2" as a duck. Recognition and recall improved clearly. Once the learners were fluent with the picture forms, I removed the images step by step until they worked with the plain symbols.

Connection to later work

The problem here was building a correspondence between a form the learner can hold and the abstract symbol underneath. AphaSpeak works on the same class of problem, with speech instead of digits.
