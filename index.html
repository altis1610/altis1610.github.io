AphaSpeak

A multimodal phone app that reconstructs what a person with non-fluent aphasia is trying to say — fusing speech, lip reading, gesture, and scene context into ranked candidate sentences.

Inversion attacks on speaker models

Gradient-based model inversion can steal a speaker's vocal traits from a trained classifier. We measured how architecture shapes that vulnerability — and when differential privacy actually helps.
