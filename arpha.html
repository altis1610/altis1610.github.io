arpha Nature Notes

A self-published rebuild of junior-high physics & chemistry.

THE PROJECT

To help my younger brother study, I rebuilt the junior-high physics and chemistry curriculum as "arpha Nature Notes" — systematizing scattered mnemonics, reordering units so concepts connect, and producing animated explainer videos to visualize the abstract parts.

WHAT IT TAUGHT ME

Learning is not about stuffing in information but about reorganizing and translating it so knowledge nodes link meaningfully — the thread that runs from these notes straight to my research on communication.
