Bug_Track — event detection for field-biology videos

Replacing weeks of manual footage review with computer vision.

GITHUB ↗

The problem it solves

A friend in the biology program was studying whether spider-web geometry affects predation efficiency — which meant weeks of camera footage reviewed by hand, with observers often disagreeing on what counted as an event. Off-the-shelf detectors like YOLO were out: outdoor lighting shifts wildly and there were too few labeled insect samples to train on.

How it works

  • Splits long videos into segments to keep memory bounded and processing stable
  • Applies image processing to suppress lighting changes before comparison
  • Computes inter-frame differences to catch motion
  • Merges consecutive above-threshold frames into single events, so one catch is not counted five times
  • A Tkinter GUI lets researchers with no CS background tune parameters and verify results

Outcome

The tool replaced most of the manual annotation and settled the between-observer disagreement — the counts stopped depending on who watched the video.
