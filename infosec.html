Teaching Lead, InfoSec Research Club

Turning cryptographic protocols into lessons people can verify.

TEACHING CRYPTO

Designed and taught the club's lecture series on cryptographic protocols — oblivious transfer, double encryption, and the millionaires' problem — using interactive simulations so members could play through each protocol instead of memorizing it.

THE METHOD

To explain a protocol I first abstract the core problem and its input/output conditions, then modularize the flow into independently analyzable sub-steps and verify the structure end to end. That decomposition habit became the backbone of how I approach research and system design.
