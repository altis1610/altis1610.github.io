Deep-Learning Reading Group

A theory-first study group I founded for junior students.

WHY IT EXISTS

Juniors who wanted to start research kept hitting the same wall: tutorials teach tools, not understanding. So I founded a weekly reading group and built the curriculum from the ground up — starting with what a neural network actually computes, before any framework code.

WHAT WE COVER

Network architectures, loss functions, and optimization, always with the underlying math derivations — gradient descent, backpropagation, parameter updates — so the theory maps onto how real models behave. My rule: in the AI era, coding can be assisted; a deep grasp of the theory is the lasting competitive edge.
