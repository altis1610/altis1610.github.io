Joint STEM Research Symposium

Presenting my research — and how research actually gets unstuck.

PRESENTER & STAFF

At the 7th inter-school joint math & science symposium I presented my own research, served on the event staff, and was later invited back as a speaker representing outstanding student researchers — talking to an auditorium of juniors about what starting research actually feels like.

THE TALK

I shared how to grow research topics from everyday observation, judge their feasibility and room to extend, and — most importantly — how to break through walls: decompose the tangled problem into verifiable sub-questions, record variables and parameter changes systematically instead of tuning from memory, and treat frustration and revision as part of the method, not a failure of it. I walked through the tools and experiment-design workflow I actually use, because a concrete pipeline beats abstract advice.

WHY I KEEP SHARING

Explaining my own process forces me to re-examine it. Every time I lay out how a project got unstuck, I see the blind spots in my own thinking more clearly — sharing has never been a one-way transfer; it's how I audit myself.
